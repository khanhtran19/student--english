## How to Use It:

1. **Paste Your Text** - Copy any English passage (article, story, essay) and paste it in the text box

2. **Analyze** - Click "Analyze & Create Flashcards" and the app will:
   - Find all meaningful words (filters out common words like "the", "is", "a")
   - Count how many times each word appears
   - Sort them by frequency (most repeated words first)

3. **Study with Flashcards**:
   - See each word with its frequency count and context from your passage
   - Click "Show Vietnamese Meaning" to get automatic Vietnamese translation
   - Choose "Study Again" if you need more practice, or "Mark as Learned" to move it to the next cycle

4. **Track Progress**:
   - Words start in Cycle 1
   - When you mark a word as learned, it moves to Cycle 2, then 3, etc.
   - Click "View Progress" to see all your words organized by cycle

The most frequently repeated words will appear first, so you learn the most important vocabulary from your passage right away!
